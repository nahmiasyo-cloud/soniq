# SONIQ

תיאוריה שרואים על הכלי ושומעים באוזן. Theory you can see on your instrument and hear with your ear.

A music theory and ear-training app for guitar, bass and piano, built as a single-page PWA that works offline.

## What's in it

- **Map**: every chord, scale, interval and arpeggio on guitar, bass and piano, including maqamat, ragas and Ethiopian qenet. Full and close chord shapes, inversions, slash chords.
- **Practice**: short 10-question rounds: notes on the neck, intervals, chords by ear, root strings. Missed items come back next round.
- **Instruments**: a playable bass neck: touch to play, drag along a string to slide, one note per string, optional mute on lift, note names, full screen.
- **Keys**: a playable two-octave keyboard: multi-touch chords, sustain while held, glissando, piano / electric piano / organ / vibraphone, octave shift, full screen.
- **Chord guitar**: pick a key, tap its chords (shown as degree and name) to strum, or swipe across the strings; acoustic or electric.
- **Drums**: a playable drum kit for your fingers, multi-touch, full screen and sideways, with softer hits at the edge, rimshot and ride bell.
- **Beats**: 16 pads for hip-hop and electronic music (two kits), a 16-step sequencer with tempo, swing and live recording, timed by the audio clock, and a sampler that records up to 3 seconds from the microphone onto any pad. Patterns and recordings stay on the device. A looper records the microphone in sync with the beat: one big button (record, play, add a layer), 1, 2 or 4 bars after a one-bar count-in, up to 8 layers, undo and clear; the take is cut on the bar lines by the audio clock, with the device's delay measured by a short calibration or estimated. Loops live in memory only.
- **Tuner**: 20 guitar and bass tunings plus a chromatic mode for any instrument, with B♭, E♭ and F transposition for winds. **Metronome**: meters and subdivisions, tap tempo.
- **Jam**: backing tracks in more than 40 styles with variants: blues, rock (incl. classic rock, krautrock, prog in 7/8), metal, surf, pop, funk, soul, hip-hop, jazz (incl. Dixieland, tom-tom swing, fusion), Latin and bossa nova, reggae, ska, dub, dancehall, afrobeat, Ethio-jazz, flamenco, bluegrass, country, electronic (house, techno, psytrance, rave, drum and bass), and Arabic, Turkish, Persian, Indian and Ethiopian rhythms. Drones, YouTube jam, your own chord progression.
- **Three levels**: beginner, intermediate, professional. Everything stays one tap away.
- **Eight languages**: Hebrew, English, Arabic, Russian, French, Spanish, German, Italian. The first run follows the phone's language.

## Accessibility

Keyboard and screen reader support, right-to-left for Hebrew and Arabic, 44 px touch targets, visible focus, no double-tap zoom on controls while pinch zoom stays available.

## Sounds

Recorded instruments are CC0 (Versilian Community Sample Library, FreePats, Karoryfer Samples). The beat kits are CC0 recordings too: the samples bundled with Sonic Pi (github.com/sonic-pi-net/sonic-pi, etc/samples; its LICENSE.md states every bundled sample is CC0 1.0, originally from freesound.org), claps, shaker and tambourine from the Versilian Community Sample Library (github.com/sgossner/VCSL, CC0 1.0), and the side stick from Karoryfer Big Rusty Drums (CC0 1.0). The exact source file of each pad is listed at the top of sounds/beats-*-mp3.js. The organ, tanpura, tabla, drones and electronic synths are synthesized in the app. Other instruments: FluidR3 GM (MIT). Style names describe genres; artist references appear only as historical context.
