# SONIQ

תיאוריה שרואים על הכלי ושומעים באוזן. Theory you can see on your instrument and hear with your ear.

A music theory and ear-training app for guitar, bass and piano, built as a single-page PWA that works offline.

## What's in it

- **Map**: every chord, scale, interval and arpeggio on guitar, bass and piano, including maqamat, ragas and Ethiopian qenet. Full and close chord shapes, inversions, slash chords.
- **Practice**: short 10-question rounds: notes on the neck, intervals, chords by ear, root strings. Missed items come back next round.
- **Tuner**: 20 guitar and bass tunings plus a chromatic mode for any instrument, with B♭, E♭ and F transposition for winds. **Metronome**: meters and subdivisions, tap tempo.
- **Jam**: backing tracks in more than 40 styles with variants: blues, rock (incl. classic rock, krautrock, prog in 7/8), metal, surf, pop, funk, soul, hip-hop, jazz (incl. Dixieland, tom-tom swing, fusion), Latin and bossa nova, reggae, ska, dub, dancehall, afrobeat, Ethio-jazz, flamenco, bluegrass, country, electronic (house, techno, psytrance, rave, drum and bass), and Arabic, Turkish, Persian, Indian and Ethiopian rhythms. Drones, YouTube jam, your own chord progression.
- **Three levels**: beginner, intermediate, professional. Everything stays one tap away.
- **Eight languages**: Hebrew, English, Arabic, Russian, French, Spanish, German, Italian. The first run follows the phone's language.

## Accessibility

Keyboard and screen reader support, right-to-left for Hebrew and Arabic, 44 px touch targets, visible focus, no double-tap zoom on controls while pinch zoom stays available.

## Sounds

Recorded instruments are CC0 (Versilian Community Sample Library, FreePats, Karoryfer Samples). The organ, tanpura, tabla, drones and electronic synths are synthesized in the app. Other instruments: FluidR3 GM (MIT). Style names describe genres; artist references appear only as historical context.
